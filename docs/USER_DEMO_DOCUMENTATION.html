Batjee / TradiGo User Functionality Guide

Step-by-step user handoff document

Purpose: This document is a text-only guide for end users. Each section explains how to perform one function inside Batjee / TradiGo.

1. Create an Account

  1. Open the website.
  2. Click the sign in or register button.
  3. Open the Register tab.
  4. Enter your full name.
  5. Enter your email address.
  6. Enter your referral code if you have one.
  7. Enter your house number, street number, area, city, postal code, and country.
  8. Enter your password.
  9. Confirm your password.
  10. Submit the registration form.

2. Log In

  1. Open the website.
  2. Click the sign in button.
  3. Enter your email address.
  4. Enter your password.
  5. Click Sign In.

3. Browse the Homepage

  1. Open the homepage.
  2. Scroll through the hero section, categories, and featured listings.
  3. Click a category or listing to continue browsing.

4. Search for a Product

  1. Open the marketplace or listings page.
  2. Enter a keyword into the search bar.
  3. Press Enter or submit the search.
  4. Review the filtered results.

5. Filter Listings by Category or Location

  1. Open the marketplace or listings page.
  2. Select a category filter if needed.
  3. Enter a location if needed.
  4. Apply the filters.
  5. Review the updated listing results.

6. Open a Product Page

  1. Open the marketplace or listings page.
  2. Click any listing card.
  3. Review the product name, price, images, description, and seller information.

7. Save a Listing to Favorites

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open a listing from the marketplace or product page.
  3. Click the favorite or heart button.
  4. Open your dashboard or favorites area to confirm it was saved.

8. Remove a Listing from Favorites

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard or favorites area.
  3. Find the saved listing.
  4. Click the remove favorite or unfavorite action.

9. Open a Seller Profile

  1. Open a product page.
  2. Click the seller profile link or seller name if available.
  3. Review the seller's listings and rating information.

10. Send a Message to a Seller

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open a product page or seller listing.
  3. Enter your message in the message field.
  4. Click Send.
  5. Open the Messages page to continue the conversation.

11. Open the Messages Inbox

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the Messages page from the navigation or mobile footer.
  3. View your conversation list.
  4. Click a conversation to open the chat thread.

12. Send an Image in Chat

  1. Open the Messages page.
  2. Open a conversation.
  3. Use the image upload option in the chat composer.
  4. Select an image from your device.
  5. Send the message with the image.

13. Open Support Chat

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the Messages page.
  3. Use the support conversation option if available.
  4. Type your concern or question.
  5. Send the message and wait for an admin reply.

14. Post a New Listing

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the Post page.
  3. Enter the listing title.
  4. Select a category.
  5. Enter the price.
  6. Enter the description.
  7. Upload one or more images.
  8. Submit the listing.
  9. Open your dashboard to track the listing status.

15. Check Listing Status

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard.
  3. Review your listing table.
  4. Check whether the listing is Pending, Active, Inactive, or Sold.

16. Withdraw a Pending Listing

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard.
  3. Find a listing with Pending status.
  4. Click Withdraw.

17. Reactivate an Inactive Listing

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard.
  3. Find a listing with Inactive status.
  4. Click the action to make it Active again.

18. Mark a Listing as Sold

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard.
  3. Find the listing you want to mark as sold.
  4. Use the sold action if it is available.

19. Use the Dashboard

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the dashboard.
  3. Review your total listings, active listings, pending listings, and sold listings.
  4. Review your favorites section.

20. Copy Your Referral Code

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard.
  3. Find your referral section.
  4. Click Copy Code.

21. Copy Your Referral Link

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your dashboard.
  3. Find your referral link.
  4. Click Copy Link.
  5. Share the copied link with another user.

22. Rate a Seller

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the seller profile page.
  3. Select a score from 1 to 5.
  4. Enter a comment if needed.
  5. Submit the rating.

23. Report a Listing

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the product page you want to report.
  3. Select the report type.
  4. Enter details about the issue.
  5. Upload an image if needed.
  6. Submit the report.

24. Report a Seller

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the seller profile page.
  3. Select the report option.
  4. Choose the report type.
  5. Enter the details.
  6. Upload an image if needed.
  7. Submit the report.

25. Open Notifications

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open the notifications area if available.
  3. Review unread and previous notifications.

26. Mark Notifications as Read

  1. Open the notifications area.
  2. Open a specific notification to mark it as read, or use the mark all as read action if available.

27. Start a Transaction in Chat

  1. Open the Messages page.
  2. Open a conversation related to a listing.
  3. Enter the agreed amount in the transaction section.
  4. Save or create the transaction.

28. Confirm the Agreed Amount

  1. Open the conversation that contains the transaction.
  2. Review the agreed amount.
  3. Use the confirm amount action.
  4. Wait for the other party to confirm as well.

29. Mark the Deal as Completed

  1. Open the conversation that contains the transaction.
  2. Use the mark completed action after the meetup or handoff is done.
  3. Wait for the other party to mark the deal completed too.

30. Submit Platform Fee Payment

  1. Open the completed transaction in the conversation.
  2. Select the fee payment action.
  3. Enter the payment method.
  4. Enter the payment reference if needed.
  5. Upload proof of payment if needed.
  6. Submit the fee payment.

31. Use the Mobile Navigation

  1. Open the website on a mobile device or narrow screen.
  2. Use the bottom navigation to open Home, Chat, Sell, My Ads, or Account.
  3. Check the Chat icon for unread message count.

32. Use Back to Top

  1. Scroll down on a desktop page.
  2. Click the Back to top button when it appears.

33. Important Notes

  • You must be logged in to post listings, send messages, save favorites, submit reports, rate sellers, and use the dashboard.
  • New listings stay pending until an admin approves them.
  • If you have unpaid platform fees, you may be blocked from posting a new listing.
